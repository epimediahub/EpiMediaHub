# Android 0.9.9 · Private original skins

The source installer builds on 0.9.8:

```bash
PROJECT_ROOT=/path/to/EpiMediaHubAndroid bash build_tools/install_android_v099_source.sh
```

The catalog has 83 password-gated original skins in twelve categories: nine
football leagues, national teams, Formula 1, and car brands. The default
EpiMediaHub theme remains an internal fallback. The four inspired luxury skins,
the two public color variants, and the old simulated marks are excluded.

The selector opens a category first, then shows its skins. Every football skin
uses the same stadium photograph, every Formula 1 skin the same pit photograph,
and every car skin the same garage photograph. These images keep their natural
colors. Original badges and restrained menu accents distinguish individual
skins. The Formula 1 assets are separate 2026 team logos, including Audi and
Cadillac, rather than car-brand aliases.

`asset_sources.json` records each downloaded mark. The three photographic
backdrops were generated without brands or emblems. Third-party logos remain
the property of their respective owners; including their sources here does not
grant publication rights.
